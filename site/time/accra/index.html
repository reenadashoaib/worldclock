Time zone

Ghana uses Greenwich Mean Time all year. The Greenwich meridian itself crosses the Ghanaian coast at Tema, just east of Accra.

Daylight saving time

Accra does not use daylight saving time, so its clocks stay the same all year. Its time difference with cities that do change their clocks, such as London and New York, shifts by an hour twice a year.
