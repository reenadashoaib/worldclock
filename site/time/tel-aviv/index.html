Time in Tel Aviv, Israel

Time zone

Time zoneIsrael Standard Time, and Israel Daylight Time in summer
UTC offsetUTC+2, and UTC+3 in summer
Daylight saving timeUsed

Israel uses Israel Standard Time, two hours ahead of GMT. Its summer time starts on the Friday before the last Sunday in March, a couple of days before Europe's, and ends on the last Sunday in October. The working week usually runs from Sunday to Thursday.

Daylight saving time

Tel Aviv uses daylight saving time. The next changes: clocks go forward one hour on Friday 26 March 2027, when 2:00 am becomes 3:00 am (UTC+3), and go back one hour on Sunday 31 October 2027, when 2:00 am becomes 1:00 am (UTC+2).
