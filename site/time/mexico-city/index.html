Mexico ended daylight saving time for most of the country in 2022, so from spring to autumn the city is one hour behind Chicago and Dallas, which still change their clocks.

Daylight saving time

Mexico City does not use daylight saving time, so its clocks stay the same all year. Its time difference with cities that do change their clocks, such as London and New York, shifts by an hour twice a year.
