Sydney is on Australian Eastern Time. New South Wales, Victoria and Tasmania use daylight saving time from the first Sunday in October to the first Sunday in April, but Queensland does not, so in summer Sydney is one hour ahead of Brisbane.

Daylight saving time

Sydney uses daylight saving time. The next changes: clocks go back one hour on Sunday 4 April 2027, when 3:00 am becomes 2:00 am (UTC+10), and go forward one hour on Sunday 3 October 2027, when 2:00 am becomes 3:00 am (UTC+11).
