Qatar uses Arabia Standard Time, three hours ahead of GMT, the same as Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Bahrain. There is no daylight saving time, and the working week runs from Sunday to Thursday.

Daylight saving time

Doha does not use daylight saving time, so its clocks stay the same all year. Its time difference with cities that do change their clocks, such as London and New York, shifts by an hour twice a year.
