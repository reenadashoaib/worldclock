Brazil stopped using daylight saving time in 2019, so the clocks in São Paulo no longer change, but the time difference with Europe and North America still shifts when those regions change theirs.

Daylight saving time

São Paulo does not use daylight saving time, so its clocks stay the same all year. Its time difference with cities that do change their clocks, such as London and New York, shifts by an hour twice a year.
