Lisbon shares its clock with London rather than with Madrid next door: Portugal uses Western European Time, one hour behind most of the European Union. The Azores islands run one hour behind mainland Portugal.

Daylight saving time

Lisbon uses daylight saving time. The next changes: clocks go forward one hour on Sunday 28 March 2027, when 1:00 am becomes 2:00 am (UTC+1), and go back one hour on Sunday 31 October 2027, when 2:00 am becomes 1:00 am (UTC+0).
