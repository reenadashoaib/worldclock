The Netherlands uses Central European Time. Until 1940 the country kept its own Amsterdam Time, about 20 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time.

Daylight saving time

Amsterdam uses daylight saving time. The next changes: clocks go forward one hour on Sunday 28 March 2027, when 2:00 am becomes 3:00 am (UTC+2), and go back one hour on Sunday 31 October 2027, when 3:00 am becomes 2:00 am (UTC+1).
