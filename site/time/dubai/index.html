Dubai is on Gulf Standard Time, four hours ahead of GMT and one hour ahead of Saudi Arabia and Qatar. The United Arab Emirates moved its official working week to Monday to Friday in 2022, closer to Europe's.

Daylight saving time

Dubai does not use daylight saving time, so its clocks stay the same all year. Its time difference with cities that do change their clocks, such as London and New York, shifts by an hour twice a year.
