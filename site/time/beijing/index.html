Time zone

Time zoneChina Standard Time
UTC offsetUTC+8
Daylight saving timeNot used

China uses a single time zone, China Standard Time, eight hours ahead of GMT, even though the country spans about five geographical time zones. In far-western Xinjiang the winter sun can rise after 9 am by the official clock.

Daylight saving time

Beijing does not use daylight saving time, so its clocks stay the same all year. Its time difference with cities that do change their clocks, such as London and New York, shifts by an hour twice a year.
