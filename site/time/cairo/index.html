Egypt uses Eastern European Time, two hours ahead of GMT. The country has started and stopped daylight saving time several times over the years, so check the dates below for the current rules.

Daylight saving time

Cairo uses daylight saving time. The next changes: clocks go forward one hour on Friday 30 April 2027, when 12:00 am becomes 1:00 am (UTC+3), and go back one hour at midnight at the end of Thursday 28 October 2027, to 11:00 pm (UTC+2).
