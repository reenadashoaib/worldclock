New York runs on Eastern Time, shared with most of the US East Coast and with Toronto and Montreal. The New York Stock Exchange opens at 9:30 am Eastern Time, which makes this the zone much of the world's financial day is measured against.

Daylight saving time

New York uses daylight saving time. The next changes: clocks go forward one hour on Sunday 14 March 2027, when 2:00 am becomes 3:00 am (UTC−4), and go back one hour on Sunday 7 November 2027, when 2:00 am becomes 1:00 am (UTC−5).
