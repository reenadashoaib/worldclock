Fiji Time is twelve hours ahead of GMT, which makes Fiji one of the first countries to start each new day. The 180° meridian passes through the Fijian islands, but the International Date Line bends around them so the whole country keeps the same date.

Daylight saving time

Suva does not use daylight saving time, so its clocks stay the same all year. Its time difference with cities that do change their clocks, such as London and New York, shifts by an hour twice a year.
