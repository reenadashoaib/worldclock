About Country Time

Country Time is a free world clock. It shows the local time in cities around the world, how far ahead or behind you each one is, and lets you shift every clock at once to find a time that works across time zones.

The clocks run entirely in your browser using its built-in time zone data, including daylight saving changes.

What you'll find here

  • The world clock, with the time in 45 major cities, a slider to plan calls across time zones, and a search covering about 34,000 cities and towns.
  • A page for each city, with its time zone, daylight saving dates and the time difference from other cities.
  • Guides to daylight saving time, scheduling across time zones, and how time zones are named.

Where the information comes from

Local times, time zones and daylight saving rules come from the IANA time zone database, the same source used by phones and computers. It is updated whenever a country changes its rules. City search uses place names from GeoNames, shared under the CC BY 4.0 licence.
